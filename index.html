Browser: Tested on Chrome and Safari (macOS/iOS). All analysis runs in your browser, and your files are never uploaded. To add names, domains and reported interactions, some pages look things up at public services (UniProt, EBI BLAST, AlphaFold DB, MIST, BioGRID): see what leaves your browser.
Reference Cutoffs: The iLIS reference cutoffs (0.223 / 0.339 / 0.551, corresponding to 10% / 5% / 1% empirical FPR) were calibrated on the top-ranked of five heterodimer models predicted with AlphaFold-Multimer v2.3 weights (ColabFold --model-type alphafold2_multimer_v3). For other predictors (AlphaFold 3, Boltz, Chai-1, etc.), single-model runs, homodimers, and multichain assemblies, these values serve as standardized reference tiers rather than exact empirical FPRs.
